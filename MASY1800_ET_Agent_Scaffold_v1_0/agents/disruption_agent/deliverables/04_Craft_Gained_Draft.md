# Craft Gained — draft for student review

I initially treated different ratings across cases as evidence of context sensitivity. Reviewing the outputs showed a subtler problem: the agent rated revenue-model exposure in one case and workflow change in another. I also learned that defining a mid-term window does not establish when disruption will arrive. Next time, I will hold the assessment object constant, require evidence for impact timing, and separate a useful pilot from a market forecast. A schema-valid response can still support the wrong management inference.
