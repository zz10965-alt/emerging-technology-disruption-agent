# Craft Gained — draft for student review

I learned that changing ratings across cases is not enough to demonstrate context sensitivity. Our initial outputs mixed revenue-model threats with workflow benefits and treated planning windows like impact evidence. The retest improved uncertainty handling, but repeated counterarguments and a shared conversation limited the result. Next time, I will hold the assessment object constant, separate a pilot schedule from a disruption forecast, and preserve unsuccessful corrections. A useful agent must expose where its judgment stops, even when its JSON validates and its prose sounds convincing.
