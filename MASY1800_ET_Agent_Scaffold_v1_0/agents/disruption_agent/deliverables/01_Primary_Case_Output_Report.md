# Primary-Case Agent Output Report

**Status:** Reviewed report of actual v0 output; revision 0.4 prepared, behavioral retest pending. This is not a report of a successful revised-agent run.

## Case and decision

The case is a hypothetical small bilingual consultancy helping Chinese home/lifestyle consumer brands enter the United States. It sells fixed-fee public-source market-entry research packages through referrals and direct outreach. Junior desk research, bilingual synthesis and senior review are material activities. These are declared scenario assumptions, not facts about an identified firm. The decision is what to do over the next 12 months with human-assisted LLM research and drafting. Legal, tax, customs and product-compliance advice are outside scope.

The unchanged technology can assist bilingual public-source synthesis; it does not itself supply real customer interviews, verified local observations or channel execution. No financial statements, client survey, willingness-to-pay test or consulting productivity measurement has been performed.

## Actual agent finding and reviewer judgment

The v0 agent returned conditional **High** Business-Model Disruption Potency and **High × Mid-term**, using October 14, 2027–October 13, 2029 as its material-impact window. Its recommendation was to prepare and pilot. The exact original is `responses/primary_v0.json`.

The business exposure is plausible: if clients can obtain reliable generic report content themselves, part of the consultancy's paid offering may lose differentiation. But neither client substitution nor material price erosion is observed in the evidence. The calendar bins were supplied as analytical conventions, not arrival forecasts. The reviewer therefore does **not** endorse an evidenced High/mid-term forecast. Supported real-world magnitude and timing remain undetermined. A High stress scenario is useful only if reliable client self-service or competing offers materially displace the paid report core. This is a conditional assessment, not a categorical downgrade to Moderate.

## Three levels of analysis

- **ET generally:** LLM task assistance is plausible, with source accuracy, privacy and human-overreliance limits. Task improvement alone does not prove Christensen-style disruption. Workplace evidence is from a different setting.
- **For this application:** Public competitor scans, bilingual briefing and first drafts may become easier to produce. AI-generated personas are not real U.S. consumer validation. Whether speed exceeds verification/rework cost is unmeasured.
- **For this organization:** Selling report content exposes a different paid value mechanism from selling field validation or consumer products. Bilingual organization and senior judgment may retain value; their defensibility and clients' willingness to pay have not been tested.

## Matrix and bounded management response

Chapter 1 Figure 1.8 maps High/mid-term to prepare/pilot, Moderate/near-term to pilot/partner and Low/near-term to a sustaining update. The v0 primary used the first cell, but material-impact timing is unsupported; a hypothetical cell must not be passed off as an evidenced placement.

Management can still justify a reversible learning pilot now. The principal and research lead should compare source-linked AI and manual briefs on de-identified/public topics, measuring factual traceability, all-in analyst plus senior-review time, rework and client usefulness. An 8–12-week pilot is a proposed schedule, not a prediction of market disruption. Establish success thresholds from a documented baseline before running it. Pause for confidentiality incidents or uncorrected material factual errors. Do not cut staff, reduce fees or make client entry commitments on these unmeasured hypotheses.

## Incumbent dilemma and escalation

Protecting billable preparation or report pricing may delay learning; premature cannibalization may reduce revenue without delivering reliable value. The better question is which work clients still need verified and will pay for. Shift effort only after measuring demand and cost, preserving human accountability and actual fieldwork. Review monthly pilot evidence and quarterly client/competitive signals. A credible escalation signal is documented substitution of equivalent verified outputs in lost/won proposals, with a defined denominator and baseline; v0's 20%/25% thresholds were uncalibrated proposals, not evidence.
